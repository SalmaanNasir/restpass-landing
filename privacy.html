Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This short policy explains how restpass handles information submitted through the current waitlist and hotel interest forms.

Information we collect

For customers joining the waitlist, we collect an email address. For hotel partnership enquiries, we collect a work email address and hotel name.

How we use it

  • Send waitlist and restpass launch updates.
  • Respond to hotel partnership enquiries.
  • Operate, secure, and improve the pre-launch website.

We do not sell personal information or use these details for unrelated advertising.

Service providers

We may use trusted service providers to host the website, store submissions, and deliver email. They may process information only to provide those services to restpass.

Retention and your choices

We keep submissions while they are needed for the waitlist, launch communications, or a hotel partnership discussion. You may ask us to stop emailing you or delete your submission at any time.

Contact

For a privacy question or deletion request, email support@restpass.lk.

We will update this policy if the website begins collecting additional information or restpass launches new services.
